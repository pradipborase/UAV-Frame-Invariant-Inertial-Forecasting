\section*{S6. Filter and artifact audit}
The anti-aliasing filters were fixed before forecasting and implemented as causal elliptic IIR filters in SOS form, with a 30 Hz passband edge, 45 Hz stopband edge, at most 1 dB passband ripple, at least 60 dB stopband attenuation, and forward-only \texttt{sosfilt}. The 200 Hz EuRoC design is fifth order and is stored in three SOS rows, one containing a first-order denominator section; the 500 Hz UZH-FPV design is sixth order. The 200 Hz design has maximum pole radius 0.943485 and a relative impulse-response tail of $4.01\times10^{-6}$ after 1 s; the 500 Hz design has maximum pole radius 0.985014 and a relative tail of $1.54\times10^{-4}$. Exact SOS coefficients are reported in the main manuscript.
